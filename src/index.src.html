How we calculate this

One printer is simulated over four weeks. The lab is staffed Monday to Friday from 9:00 for the hours you set, and a finished print waits about 15 minutes before someone clears it. Outside those hours it waits until the next morning. The WorkCell swaps plates in under 60 seconds, runs 2 printers per cell and can work through 15 plates before someone needs to empty the shelves. Costs in the comparison below use $80,000 per 4-printer Mosaic Array (2023 list price) and Prusa’s announced starting price before VAT.
